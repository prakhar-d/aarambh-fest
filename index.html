About the University

Jaipur National University

Jaipur National University (JNU) is a self-financed private university established by the Government of Rajasthan under the Jaipur National University Act, 2008. It is sponsored by the Mahima Shiksha Samiti and sits on approximately 50 acres at Jagatpura, off the Jaipur-Agra Bypass.

  • UGC-recognised and NAAC accredited
  • Three well-developed campuses with world-class infrastructure
  • 15 minutes from Jaipur International Airport
  • AARAMBH has become one of Jaipur's most anticipated student festivals
